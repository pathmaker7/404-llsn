Record the film voice

Read each line in your own voice. Every line has a time it must fit in (it starts when its caption appears in the film). The yellow bar fills over that time while you speak; finish before it turns red.

  1. Use Chrome, a quiet room, headphones off the desk, the laptop mic about 20 cm from your mouth. Allow the microphone when asked.
  2. Press Record (or the Space key), wait half a second, read the line calmly and clearly, press Stop (or Space). Silence at the start and end is cut off automatically.
  3. Green means it fits. Red means read a little faster or redo it. Press Play to listen, Redo to try again. Only the last take of each line is kept.
  4. When all eight are green, press Download my voice and tell Claude "recorded". The file stays on your Mac; this page uploads nothing.
