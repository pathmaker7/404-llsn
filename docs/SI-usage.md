# Use of AI (SI) tools

Space Apps asks teams to say where they used AI. We did, and here is where.

| What | Tool | How the team checked it |
|---|---|---|
| The 3D view, the terrain pipeline, the horizon masks | Claude (Anthropic), directed by teammate steppekid | his pipeline tests, and a comparison with NASA JPL Horizons |
| The answer panel, the survival rules, positions for 2021 to 2031, the tests | Claude (Anthropic) in Claude Code, directed by the team | `npm test`, `npm run smoke`, and the team playing the app |
| README, the "How it works" page, the Russian and Kyrgyz texts | Claude (Anthropic) | read by the team; Russian and Kyrgyz need a native read before the online presentation |
| Four games for students: "My lander" (`app/src/lander.js`, `app/src/lander-models.js`), "Keep me alive" (`app/src/game.js`), "Ice hunter" (`app/src/ice.js`), "Quiz" (`app/src/quiz.js`), and the help for a first visit (`app/src/tour.js`: the tour and the "How to play" cards), each with its texts file (`app/i18n-*.js`) and its test (`tools/test-*.cjs`) | Claude (Anthropic) in Claude Code, directed by the team: the team chose the four ideas, the Central Asian hats and the rule that every verdict must come from the app's own survival rule; Claude wrote the code and the tests | each feature has a browser test that compares what the student sees with the app's own verdicts (`node tools/test-lander.cjs`, `test-game.cjs`, `test-ice.cjs`, `test-quiz.cjs`, `test-tour.cjs`), plus `npm test` |
| The lander models and the four hats (Kyrgyz ak kalpak, Kazakh kalpak, Uzbek doppi, Tajik toqi) | drawn in code with Claude's help: three.js shapes and patterns drawn on a canvas, no downloaded model, no photo, no generated picture, no real person | to be looked at by the team in the app; the patterns are simplified drawings, so a teammate from each culture should say whether they look right |
| The quiz questions and answers, and the texts of the four features in Russian and Kyrgyz | drafted with Claude (Anthropic) | the English text is to be read by the team; the Russian and Kyrgyz wording is a draft until the team's native speakers have checked it (that check is still to be done) |
| The seven slide project demo (`explainer/deck.html`, printed to `explainer/deck.pdf`, where the scenes are replayed as a flipbook of pictures taken of that same page by `tools/deck-animate.cjs`) | text, layout and the animated scenes of the seven slides (SVG drawn in code, using the app's own lander drawings) written with Claude (Anthropic), directed by the team; the screenshots inside the scenes are parts of the real app; the Moon and the Earth on slide 1 are not AI pictures: they are NASA's own maps (the two the app uses, credited in `app/ATTRIBUTION.md`) wrapped on a globe by `tools/deck-globes.cjs`, and the slide says "Moon and Earth pictures: NASA"; the tab icon of both sites (`app/favicon.svg`) is a drawing written with Claude | every slide carries the line "Made with AI (SI) tools: Claude"; `npm test` fails if the deck has more or fewer than seven slides |
| The 29.5 second film on the "How it works" page (`docs/demo-30s.mp4`; it is not the project demo) | recorded from the real app by a script (`tools/record-demo.cjs`); captions written with Claude; the voice is synthetic, ElevenLabs text to speech, the voice "Sarah", reading the captions (`tools/narrate.cjs`) | carries a visible "Made with AI (SI) tools: Claude. Synthetic voice." watermark, and its file metadata names the synthetic voice, as the rules require for video and audio |

No NASA logo, flag or mission identifier is used. No image, sound or text in the app was generated as a picture or audio by AI; the Moon surface is drawn from NASA LOLA elevation data and NASA's LROC colour map of the Moon, and the landers and hats of the students' games are drawn by code, not generated as pictures. The students' games show no photo, voice or likeness of any person. The four characters on the slides (a lander, a rover, a hopper drone and a satellite) are drawings written as code with Claude's help, not generated pictures.
