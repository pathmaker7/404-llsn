# Demo film script (29 seconds)

The film is recorded from the real app by `node tools/record-demo.cjs` (`npm run video`), so the captions below are
the exact on-screen text. Order follows the Space Apps submission guide: who we are, the challenge, the title, how it
works with NASA data, what it does for people. No voice and no person appear in it (no one under 18 may appear).
A visible "Made with AI (SI) tools" watermark stays in the top right corner the whole time.

| Time | On screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:02.5 | The whole Moon, gliding to the south pole | Manuchehr, Aruzhan, Kairat, Arsen, Amirbek: team 404 agents, Naryn |
| 0:02.5 to 0:05 | The Moon is dragged and turns | Challenge: Lunar Landing Site Navigator. At the Moon's south pole one hill hides the Sun for days. |

## If you record a spoken version instead

The guide allows speech in another language only with English subtitles; the captions above are those subtitles.
Read at a calm pace, one line per row, about 2.5 words per second:

1. "We are team 404 agents from Naryn."
2. "At the Moon's south pole, one hill can hide the Sun for days."
3. "Our app answers: will a solar robot survive a landing here, on this day?"
4. "It is built on NASA terrain and JPL ephemerides. Click a day and stand on the site."
5. "One answer, eleven checks."
6. "Watch the Sun slide along the horizon."
7. "Launch slipped? Pick another day in one click."
8. "We replayed Chandrayaan-3: our sunset matches the day India put it to sleep."
9. "For planners, teachers and anyone curious."

Keep the film at 30 seconds or less: a longer film is not eligible for Global Judging.
